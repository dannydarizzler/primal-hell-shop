🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who we are

The Primal Hell Shop ("we", "us") is operated as part of the Primal Hell ARK: Survival Ascended community Discord server. This page explains what data we collect when you use the Shop and why.

2. What we collect

  • Discord User ID — used to identify your account and to know who to deliver in-game items to. We do not collect your Discord email, phone number, or other Discord account details.
  • Password — stored only as a salted cryptographic hash. We cannot see or recover your actual password.
  • Display name — automatically synced from your Discord username (via the community's Discord bot) so it always matches your real Discord identity. It is not something you can freely type or change yourself, and is shown only to you and to server staff.
  • Purchase & balance history — Coin balance, purchases, chest openings, Lucky Wheel spins, and redeemed promo codes, so we can track what you've bought and what's still owed to you in-game.
  • Payment data — when you top up Coins with PayPal, PayPal processes your payment directly. We only receive the order ID, amount, and payment status — we never see or store your card, bank, or PayPal login details.
  • Session cookie — a small signed cookie that keeps you logged in. It contains no personal data beyond your Discord ID and expires automatically.
  • "Online now" presence — while a Shop tab is open, your browser sends a small signal every 20 seconds so staff can see how many people are using the Shop right now. If you are logged in, staff can see your display name in this "who's online" list for as long as you're active; the moment you close the tab or go inactive for about a minute, you're removed from it and nothing is written to a database. If you are not logged in, this signal is a fully anonymous random ID that is never linked to any account.

3. What we don't collect

We don't collect your real name, email address, physical address, or any government ID. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers, and we don't sell or share your data with advertisers.

4. How we use your data

Solely to operate the Shop: authenticating your account, crediting Coins, fulfilling purchases in-game, preventing abuse (e.g. Lucky Wheel cooldowns, promo code limits), and letting our Discord bot notify you about purchases, Lucky Wheel wins, and redeemed codes.

5. Third parties

PayPal processes all real-money payments under its own privacy policy. Discord is used to identify your account and to send you DM notifications via our bot; Discord's own privacy policy applies to your use of Discord itself.

6. Data retention & deletion

Your account data is kept for as long as your Shop account exists. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting a staff member on Discord.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Message @DM7OP on Discord.
